The two loops

The word training means two different things here. One kind trains the market model. The other kind trains a strategy against a market that exists. Most people need only the second kind.

What the envelope adds

The preset carries a measurement with it. pt-v12 holds all fourteen realism statistics inside their real-market bands at the certified horizon of 252 trading days. It also names five gaps where it does not hold. A Loop B result gets both. The certification lets you say the market reproduced those fourteen habits of real equities. The gaps stop you before you say more than that.
