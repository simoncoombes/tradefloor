Measurement and limits

This shows one draw's consequence under the same generator: the market jump's uniform, at one address, on one day. It cannot show that day's other draws (the company jumps, the volume state, the news draws) or a trace of any of them. World.unfire in python/tradefloor/counterfactual.py runs the same surgery with a recorded log and a check that it landed where it was aimed; noise.attribute decomposes a day's price move into its causes. Both go further than this page does.

Most shipped presets set the market jump's intensity to zero, so unfiring a day that was never going to fire changes nothing: the two digests below agree, and that is a correct answer, not a broken page. The preset here is the first shipped one with a nonzero intensity.
