API

Realism and Atlas

Measuring what the market reproduces, pricing what it does not, and mapping what it responds to.

pretium.facts

measure(seed=, universe=, days=252,
        macro=None, scenario=None,
        model=None)		Run a market and report the fourteen-statistic panel. Carries model and universe fingerprints. A statistic that cannot be measured comes back None, never zero.
REAL_MARKETS · SEED_SDThe real-market bands with provenance, and each statistic's across-seed noise at the shipped baseline.
band_distance(value, low, high)Zero inside the band, distance to the nearer edge outside.
compare_to_real_markets(facts) · report(facts)Per-statistic verdicts, as data and as a printed table. Refuses to aggregate into one score.

pretium.loss

What each of the fourteen does in the loss

Each statistic has one of three roles, and the roles are one tuple each in pretium.loss. The third is derived as the complement of the first two, so promoting a statistic is a one-tuple edit and a statistic added to facts.REAL_MARKETS is excluded and reported by default rather than silently optimised against.

pretium.envelope

check(horizon_days=, statistics=(),
      sector_concentrated=False,
      scenario_magnitude=False)		Can this market answer the question? Returns a Verdict, falsy when outside, every reason naming its measurement. Unknown statistic names raise.
intervals(panels) · report_intervals(rows)Per-statistic spread across seeds beside median and band. Refuses fewer than two panels: a spread over one observation is not a spread.
score(panel, horizon_days=)A measured panel against the bands for its own horizon, with room_sd per statistic. Guards against scoring one horizon with another's ruler.
regressions(panel)Statistics the shipped preset holds in band and this panel does not. Empty means no regression against what ships.
GAPS · certified()The five measured gaps with what each forbids, and the whole list as a plain mapping for a manifest.
CERTIFIED_HORIZON_DAYS · PRESET252, and pt-v12. If your installed preset differs, check() says so rather than answering.

axes_for(names, preset="pt-v3", ranges=None, log=())Axes around shipped values. Refuses zero-shipped parameters without an explicit range, and typo'd override names.
plan(axes, samples, seed)The vectors a survey would measure, before spending anything.
survey(axes, measure, samples)Measure every planned vector. Errors are recorded rather than fatal.
Survey.sensitivity · profile · unidentified · pareto · attribution · explain · report_front · confirmThe analysis surface. Every numeric result carries its row counts and drop tally. See the Atlas reference.
Survey.save(path) · load(path) · provenance()Measure once, ask many questions later, and what the map stands on.
