REFERENCE

Under the Hood

How the simulator actually works: the Rust and Python split, the three layers of the price model, the book, the macro chain, the random number architecture, and why the determinism guarantee holds to the last bit.

Rust Core, Python API

Everything that runs a market lives in the Rust crate: the engine, the price model, the order book, the economy, the RNG and the vendored maths. The Python package re-exports it and adds the parts better written in Python: JSON round-trips, process pools for seed sweeps, and the analysis modules (facts, loss, envelope, atlas, tca). Forcing those into the extension would mean hand-rolling JSON escaping and reimplementing a process pool, both worse than the standard library versions.

Where a Decision Lives

The day loop now lives in the core: the macro step, the day close, and the mapping of initial state. Previously parts of it sat in the bindings, which meant the browser surface and the Python surface each ran their own copy.

The rule behind that move is worth stating, because it is what keeps two surfaces honest: if it decides something about the market, it belongs in the core. A binding that re-implements a modelling decision is a fork wearing the costume of glue code, and the divergence stays invisible until a whole simulated market has drifted.

How a Price Is Built

Layer 1: fair value from fundamentals. Each instrument gets a true value computed from its fundamentals, discounted at the prevailing rates. This anchor moves: when the central bank's meeting calendar moves the discount rate, fair value reprices per instrument. The one exception is a loss-maker valued off book value, which carries no discount rate.

Layer 2: an autoregressive mispricing process. The market price wanders around fair value through a mispricing term with mean reversion, momentum and a herding component, plus news shocks. This wedge between price and value is the ground truth the truth table records, and its momentum term is what makes returns trend. Under pt-v1 that reads +0.249 at lag one against a real band around zero; the shipped pt-v12 turns the term down to 0.0186 and measures +0.0239, inside the band. The herding term is load-bearing for the model's identity, and the counterfactual is one line: ModelParams.from_preset("pt-v1", momentum_theta=0.05) measured in band, honestly fingerprinted as custom.

Layer 3: GJR-GARCH volatility with a shared market factor. Every name runs a GJR-GARCH variance process, so volatility clusters and reacts asymmetrically to losses, which is where the leverage effect comes from: a symmetric process produces none at any coefficients. On top sits a shared market factor with its own conditional variance, coupled to VIX. The factor's variance reverts to a target scaling with (VIX/15)², so a pinned VIX moves realised volatility: 31% annualised at VIX 5, 37% at 15, 105% at 45, 126% at 65. Above VIX 25.5 the cross-section blends toward the factor, and mean pairwise correlation reads +0.20 calm, +0.62 at VIX 45, +0.68 at 65. Diversification fails under stress the way real crises make it fail. Since the 2026-08-26 era boundary the coupling is not the factor's alone: a name's own GJR-GARCH variance follows the VIX too (garch_vix_coupling 0.3), the sector draw's variance follows the regime in full (sector_vix_coupling 1.0, which was a quarter under pt-v10 and was quadrupled by pt-v11's crisis work), the VIX's own fear channel reads the day's index return rather than the closing minute, the volatility regimes come off the market instead of the business cycle, and the factor's variance clamp sits at 32x its baseline rather than 8x. Descriptions of these mechanics written for pt-v3 do not carry over. Universe.random(20-25, seed=11) · 120 days · sim seed 3, VIX pinned. Those levels straddle the certified panel's 32.76% rather than sitting near it, the two calm pins bracketing it and the two crisis pins running three to four times higher, because a pinned VIX is not a normal market: the pin drives the factor variance directly, and these runs use a smaller roster over 120 days rather than the certified 40 names over 252. The ratio between two of them is also not the crisis lever the realism page quotes, which is measured on the certified roster. Compare pins against each other, and take a headline volatility number from an unpinned run. See Realism and Limits.

The Order Book

Matching runs against a limit order book with price-time priority. You get queue position, partial fills, and a quoted spread that widens under stress. Impact is emergent: a large order pays worse prices because it consumed levels, and there is no slippage coefficient anywhere in the code. Read that as a statement about the mechanism, not about calibration: the realism envelope measures daily price, correlation and volume statistics, and nothing in it grades depth shape, cancellation intensity, queue dynamics, the intraday spread distribution, impact decay, resiliency or order-sign autocorrelation against real market data. The book is real and it is not certified. Session circuit breakers halt a name when a move binds the breaker, and the golden tests pin that behaviour tick by tick. The measured cost of trading too often is in Running a market.

The Macro Chain

A full Taylor/Phillips/Okun loop advances at every day close: economy update, cycle transition, central bank. VIX takes a new value every day. The policy fields step at the central bank's meeting calendar: over one measured 120-day run, the funds rate took 2 distinct values, the corporate bond yield 3, inflation 4, GDP growth 6, and fair value repriced per instrument on the meeting days the discount rate moved, days 45 and 96 of that run. A pin overrides the endogenous step from the day it lands. Universe.random(20, seed=11) · sim seed 42 · run_days(120)

RNG Streams

One seed drives the whole simulation, through seven independent substreams, so changing what one consumer draws cannot shift any other consumer's sequence. Four of the seven exist because of that rule rather than in spite of it: a mechanism that consumes draws could not have been added to a shared stream at all without shifting every later draw and moving every shipped preset's trajectory, so jumps, volume, news and per-name volume each got a stream of their own and each arrived inert.

StreamIdServes
market0every draw in the tick: market factor, sector factors, per-company noise, volume, book settlement
economy1the daily macro chain: economy update, cycle transition, central bank
external2the embedder, through Engine.draw_uniform() / draw_normal()
jumps3endogenous jumps, drawn once per name per day at the close
volume4the common persistent volume component, drawn once per day at the close
news5endogenous company news, drawn once per name per day at the open
volume_idio6per-name volume persistence, drawn once per name per day

Why the split exists. TCA re-runs the market without your orders, and the subtraction is exact only if the untraded world sees the identical noise. The macro chain's draw count genuinely varies with macro state: a chain in contraction rolls a shock the expansion never draws, so it needs its own stream. And a host application that takes one random number of its own must never invalidate a seeded market. The market stream's schedule is a pure function of market status, active roster and sector count, so no price, no macro value and no order flow can move its position. Engine.draws_by_stream() reports the counts for the first three, which are the ones a TCA subtraction compares; the four later streams are diagnosed through the snapshot instead, where each occupies three of the 21 numbers in state_snapshot()["rng"].

The derivation is a contract, pinned by a golden test against hand-computed values:

DERIVATION 
mixed      = splitmix64_mix((s << 32) | k)    # on 64 bits
seed_k     = mixed >> 32                      # top 32 bits
sequence_k = 256 + k
stream_k   = GameRng(seed_k, sequence_k)      # PCG-XSH-RR 64/32

Two properties make the streams independent. Each stream gets its own PCG sequence, hence its own odd increment, and two PCG32 generators with different increments traverse different orbits: one can never be a time-shifted copy of the other, by construction. And the seed is passed through an avalanche mixer first, because seeding two streams with the same raw state leaves a deterministic cross-stream relation that the output permutation only obscures. The mixer gives every stream an unrelated starting state. Everything in the derivation is integer arithmetic, exactly specified on every platform, so it adds nothing for the bit-identity claim to trip on.

Why the Crate Bans std Transcendentals

Rust's f64::cos delegates to the platform's maths library: MSVC's on Windows, glibc's on Linux, Apple's on macOS. They give different answers. Measured, one operation out of four differs:

OpRust std vs V8
lnidentical
sqrtidentical (IEEE-754 specifies it exactly)
sinidentical
cosdiffers by 1 ULP

One function, one bit, and one bit is enough. Normal draws route through cos, they feed the GARCH noise term for every company on every tick, and price = fair value × exp(s) is a non-linear feedback loop, so a last-bit difference becomes a different market within a simulated year. The same disagreement exists between browsers: measured across 60,000 recorded normal draws, Chrome's answers differ from a platform-libm build on 1.545% of draws, with the first divergence at draw 12. A parity test that checked six draws passed by stopping six short of the first mismatch. A test can pass because it is too small, and that is what it looks like.

So the crate vendors its maths: exp, log, pow, sin and cos come from the libm crate, a pure-Rust port of MUSL's implementation, the same code everywhere it compiles. No std transcendental reaches the source, and a test greps the source to enforce that rather than trusting anyone to remember.

What has been measured, by commit: identical digests on Windows x86_64 and macOS arm64 at a5afd1c; identical digests on all five targets (Linux x86_64 and aarch64, macOS arm64 and x86_64, Windows x86_64) at ad91026; the current era's baseline regenerated and confirmed on macOS arm64 only, until the gate runs again. The gate has not yet run against a tagged release. Read cross-platform bit-identity as measured at ad91026 and as engineering intent for the current baseline.

Performance

A 252-day year over 100 instruments takes 27 seconds on the documentation's reference desktop and under seven on an Apple-silicon laptop. Recording 9.8 million rows of ground truth costs less than wall-clock timing resolves: measured as interleaved pairs of recorded and plain runs, nearly half the pairs come out negative. Sweeps parallelise 3 to 4 times on eight cores, and runs stream a batch per day, so a hundred-seed sweep at tick grain never has to fit in memory.
